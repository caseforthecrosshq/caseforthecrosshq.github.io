Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Case for the Cross makes short videos and posts them to its own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account. This policy covers the small posting app the channel uses to do that, and this website.

Who uses the app

Only the channel's owner. The app is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given once through Google's and Meta's own sign-in screens, the app reaches only Case for the Cross's own accounts:

  • YouTube: to upload the channel's videos, schedule when they go public, and read back whether they were published.
  • Instagram (through Meta's API): to upload and publish the account's Reels and read back whether they were published.

It reads nothing else: no viewers' data, no comments or messages, and no other person's account.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in tokens and a record of what was posted and when are kept only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses the YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

This website

This site sets no cookies and collects no information. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Removing access and deleting data

The app's access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings and from Meta's business settings. Withdrawing it stops all further access at once, and the stored sign-in token can then be deleted from the owner's computer. The posting record holds only the channel's own posts (each video's id, link, title, time and status), and the owner may delete it at any time. No data received from Google is kept longer than the posting needs.

To ask about or request deletion of any data, write to the contact address below; requests are answered within 30 days.
